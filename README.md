# ☕ Coffee Sales Dashboard

Excel dashboard analyzing coffee sales across products, customers, and countries — 1,000 orders, 2019–2022.

# Contents
Dashboard – Visual summary
Total Sales – Monthly sales by coffee type
CountryBarChart – Sales by country
Top5Customers – Top 5 customers
orders – Raw order data
customers – Customer details
products – Product catalog
# Key Insights
US drives most sales, followed by Ireland and UK.
Top 5 customers are closely matched ($278–$317) — no single dominant buyer.
Covers 4 coffee types, 3 roast levels, multiple sizes.
